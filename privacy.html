LifeRoad Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

LifeRoad is a small game for unwinding after the day. It is made by marionlabs. This policy covers the LifeRoad app on Android and iPhone and the web version at liferoad-app.github.io/liferoad-web.

The short version

LifeRoad does not collect your personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • What you make in the game: the flowers you pick, your notes and pages, your garden, the star's progress and your Stardust.
  • Small settings, such as reminder times and sound on or off.

This is stored only on your phone (or in your browser, for the web version). We never receive it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

Camera

The Rainmaker game can use the camera to notice how much you move. Each frame is examined on your device and immediately thrown away. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The camera is only used while you play Rainmaker, and only if you allow it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, your phone schedules them itself. No server is involved.

Sharing

If you choose to share something from the app, your phone's own share menu sends it to the app you pick. We do not see it.

Google Play and the App Store

When you download LifeRoad, Google or Apple handle the download and may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not receive any personal data from them.

Children

LifeRoad is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that leaves your device, such as an optional account to sync between devices, we will update this page before that version is released and say exactly what is stored and where.
